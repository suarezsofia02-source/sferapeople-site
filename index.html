Q&A

Which roles do you hire for?+

We hire across engineering, data, product, design, sales, marketing, operations and G&A, from individual contributors to managers and executives. Engineering and data are where we go deepest, but every search follows the same steps: a clear brief, structured screening and a shortlist you can act on.

Where do you source candidates?+

We source wherever the role requires, with deep reach across Latin America (including Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Mexico and Uruguay), US and Europe. We combine direct outreach with our own talent pool, so you meet people beyond those actively applying.

How do you vet candidates?+

Every candidate is screened by a recruiter on experience, motivation, expectations and communication before you see them. For engineering and data roles, a specialist also runs a technical interview, so your team only meets people who have already been assessed.

What if a hire does not work out?+

Our placements come with a replacement guarantee. The terms depend on each client and their needs, and we write them into the proposal before we start. If a hire does not work out within the agreed period, we rerun the search at no extra fee.

Who handles contracts and payroll?+

Your company handles contracts and payroll. We focus on the hiring side: finding, screening and presenting candidates and guiding the process through to the offer. If you are hiring in a country where you have no entity, we can point you to the options that fit your setup.

How does pricing work?+

Pricing depends on the role, the seniority and the scope of the work. After a short call we send a clear proposal with scope and fees, so you know what to expect before we start. There is no commitment until you approve it.
