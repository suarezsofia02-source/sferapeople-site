Q&A

Which roles do you hire for?+

We cover engineering, design, product, data, sales and operations. Tech is where we go deepest.

Where do you source candidates?+

Across Latin America, mainly Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Mexico and Uruguay.

How do you vet technical candidates?+

Every tech profile is pre-screened, then interviewed technically by a specialist before you meet them.

What if a hire does not work out?+

Every placement includes a 30-day replacement guarantee. If a hire leaves or does not work out within 30 days of starting, we rerun the search at no extra fee.

Who handles contracts and payroll?+

We focus on recruiting. For contracts and payroll, we can point you to the right option for your setup.

How does pricing work?+

It depends on the role and the company. After a short call we send a clear proposal with scope and fees, so you know what to expect.
