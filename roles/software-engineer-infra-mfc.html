About the role

Own the infrastructure and backend foundations of a mobile-first SaaS product used daily by many businesses. You will work side by side with the CTO on the present and the future of the platform.

What you will do

  • Design and run scalable, reliable backend systems and the infrastructure behind them.
  • Plan infrastructure and systems design, build CI/CD pipelines and deployment processes, including monitoring and rollback.
  • Work directly with cloud services (AWS, Supabase, Cloudflare) and shape data workflows.
  • Create tools and libraries that make the whole team faster.

What we are looking for

  • 4+ years building and maintaining scalable systems at product companies. We work with Kotlin, Java, Go and Python, and the language is not a filter.
  • You have designed or led deployment pipelines, monitoring and rollback plans, and can explain what you decided yourself.
  • Solid database design, management and optimization.
  • Experience monitoring a whole architecture with tools like New Relic or Datadog.
  • Experience with AI is a strong plus.
  • You move fast, take ownership, and communicate clearly in English.

What we offer

Hybrid in Buenos Aires. Competitive salary in USD or local equivalent, equity, team retreats and a laptop and setup of your choice.
